The games

Every hole's a bet.

Pick one or stack three. Set a stake on each and Trunk keeps every one of them straight at the same time, gross or net.

The classic

Nassau

Front, back and total. 1v1 or 2v2, with presses when somebody's down and wants out.

Skins

Win the hole outright or it carries. Gross or net.

Wolf

Pick a partner, go lone, or call it blind off the tee.

Vegas

2v2, scores written side by side. Optional birdie flip.

Match play

1v1, or 2v2 best-ball.

Stableford

Points per hole. Blow-ups cost you one hole, not the round.

Quota

Beat your own points target, set by your handicap.

Stroke play

Low score takes it. Gross or net.

Scramble

Teams of two to four, one ball, a tee-shot minimum and a shotgun start.

Thursday nights

Golf League

A and B singles plus a combined team match, scored in league points across the season.

How it works

One phone. One tap each.

Set the game

Add the group and their handicaps. Search for the course, load one you saved, or scan the printed card for pars and stroke indexes.

Tap the scores

One scorekeeper, one tap per player. The money ticker at the top moves the moment a hole is in.

Settle up

Trunk boils every bet down to the fewest payments that square the group. Send the board to the group chat.

The Board

The money, live.

Nobody has to do sums on the 14th tee. The Board shows who's up, who's down and which game did it, after every hole.

  • A running total per player: +$71 −$61
  • Each game broken out, so the argument has a number in it
  • Stakes editable mid-round, when the back nine needs more on it

Settling up

Handicaps

Net, done by the book.

Enter a Handicap Index and Trunk works out what each player gets on this course, then drops the strokes on the right holes.

  • Strokes fall by stroke index, with a second on the hardest holes when the handicap runs past eighteen
  • Match play and Nassau follow the Rules of Handicapping: low man plays off scratch
  • Four-ball allowances cut to 90%, the way the book says
